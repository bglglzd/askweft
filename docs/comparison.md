# Scope and related products

Checked 2026-11-11. These public pages describe advertised scope, not tested performance.

| Product | Published scope | Askweft's bounded scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Orbii Intake](https://www.orbii.ai/orbii-intake) / [Intelligent LOS](https://www.orbii.ai/intelligent-los) | Extracts/cross-checks intake documents and gathers case-specific information through conversation. | Explicit finite alternatives, exact answer partitions and a declared question/field cost; no lending workflow. |
| [AIMuhaseb](https://aimuhaseb.com/bank-reconciliation-software-uae/) | Statement import, matching and unmatched/duplicate/missing entries. | Clarification after extraction, persisted answers and unresolved alternatives; no accounting ledger or live bank feed. |

Question selection, active learning and reconciliation already exist. Askweft makes a small
selection rule inspectable and reproducible; it claims neither global uniqueness nor
superiority over these products. The name had no inspected GitHub repository match on the
research date; this is not trademark clearance.

The greedy benefit/cost idea has research precedent, including
[Golovin, Krause and Ray](https://las.inf.ethz.ch/files/golovin10near.pdf). Askweft uses its own
uniform, noiseless finite-set objective and does not inherit theoretical guarantees from
that work. Extracted predictions and operator confirmations may be wrong in real workflows.
